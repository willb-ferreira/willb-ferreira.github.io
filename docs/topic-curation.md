# Public research-topic editorial policy

Only broad study and collaboration directions are listed here. Unpublished technical hypotheses, proof ideas, simulation plans, exact distribution constructions and partner-specific plans must not be kept in this public repository.

The site now displays six undergraduate, five master's and three doctoral broad directions and three general collaboration interests. They are not funded vacancies or evidence of established technical claims. Specific scientific plans are discussed selectively once mutual interest and appropriate access have been established.

Past public commits included more detailed topic notes. Removing those details from the current branch does not make that historical disclosure confidential. Keep future restricted material in a separate private repository or institutionally approved access-controlled space. Read `SECURITY.md` before publication.
