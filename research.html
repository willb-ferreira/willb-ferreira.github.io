Interests that may lead to joint work

These are broad interests I would be glad to discuss with colleagues and students. Specific projects depend on a good question, suitable data, time, and methodological feasibility.

Applied studies1

Remote sensing in environmental and health studies

Interest in collaborations combining remote-sensing data, spatial analysis, and domain expertise on environmental or population-level questions.

Feasibility question: The question, data, and methodological feasibility would be defined with collaborators.

Methodological research1

Modeling and inference for spatial and spatio-temporal data

Interest in methodological questions involving dependence, coherent model construction, and uncertainty quantification.

Feasibility question: Any project depends on a well-defined scientific formulation and verifiable assumptions.

Scientific software1

Scientific computing for spatial inference

Interest in reproducible software and critical comparisons of computational methods for spatial statistical models.

Feasibility question: Methods and implementation details would depend on the model and the team.
