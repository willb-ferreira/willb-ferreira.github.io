Willams Batista

Assistant Professor of Statistics · UFPE

I am a professor in the Department of Statistics at UFPE. My research covers statistical inference, models for dependent data, and statistical methods for SAR and PolSAR imagery, combining theory, simulation, and remote-sensing applications.
