FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a complete proposal?

No. A well-founded interest, an initial question, and willingness to learn are enough for a first conversation.

Can I suggest a different topic?

Yes. Proposals related to the research areas may be considered depending on scientific fit and availability.

Is funding or a position available?

No scholarship or position is advertised on this website. Availability must be confirmed individually.
